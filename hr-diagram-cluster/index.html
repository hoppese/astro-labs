Cluster Ages from the Turnoff Point

All the stars in a cluster form at once. Drag the age slider and watch the most massive stars leave the main sequence first — the turnoff point is the cluster's clock.

As the cluster ages, stars near the turnoff leave the main sequence, swell into red giants in the upper right, and eventually fade as white dwarfs in the lower left.
