Intro Astronomy Sims

24 draft sims, a companion to the intro-physics sims

Single-file interactive simulations for an introductory (non-majors) astronomy course. Same house style as the physics sims: an Info panel, a Worked example, an inputs → results layout, and everything self-contained so a page can be dropped into Moodle with an <iframe>.

Earth, Moon & sky

Moon Phases
Sun, Moon and Earth in a tilted 3D orbit view (drag the Moon); the same phase seen from the ground, with rise and set times.
Draft
Seasons
Axial tilt, not distance: a 3D orbit, a day-and-night globe, noon Sun altitude, day length and the real Earth–Sun distance.
Draft
Eclipses
Solar vs lunar in a draggable 3D view plus a to-scale side view of the shadows; the 5° tilt and the nodes explain why not every month.
Draft
The Celestial Sphere
A draggable 3D sky sphere with your horizon (or a flat map): daily motion, altitude and azimuth, circumpolar stars, changing latitude.
Draft
Retrograde Motion
Mars looping against stars in the southern sky, with numbered snapshots linking each loop point to the inside lane passing the outside lane.
Draft
Phases of Venus
Elongation, phase and apparent size changing together; configurations and synodic period; why Ptolemy’s epicycle can’t make a gibbous Venus.
Draft

Orbits & gravity

Kepler's Laws
Ellipse shape, the equal-areas sweep, and P² = a³ checked against the real planets.
Draft
Tides
The Sun and Moon’s real tidal-force vectors raising two bulges; spring vs neap, with tide heights in metres.
Draft
Exoplanet Transit
Build a light curve with limb darkening; a side view shows inclination; depth = (Rp/R*)², duration, period.

Radial Velocity
A star wobbling around the barycentre in a 3D orbit, with the line of sight and inclination i; its spectral lines shift red and blue in step.

Light & stars

Blackbody Spectrum
Planck curve vs temperature, Wien’s law for the peak, and the colour a star ends up looking.
Draft
Atomic Spectra
Bohr energy levels and the photons they emit or absorb; Kirchhoff’s three spectrum types; identify a gas by its lines.
Draft
Telescopes
What aperture buys: light-gathering ∝ D², resolution 1.22λ/D on a double star, and why magnification alone doesn’t help.
Draft
Stellar Parallax
A nearby star’s yearly shift in 3D and on a true-scale sky, at any ecliptic latitude; distance in pc = 1 / parallax in arcsec.
Draft
Inverse-Square Brightness
Flux falling as 1/d² on the 1-4-9 squares; apparent vs absolute magnitude, the distance modulus and the naked-eye limit.
Draft
H-R Diagram
L = 4πR²σT⁴: place a star by temperature and luminosity, with spectral subtypes and white-dwarf types.
Draft
Spectral Classification
The OBAFGKM types and the I–V luminosity classes as bands on the H–R diagram; read off a star’s absolute magnitude.
Draft

Stellar lives & pairs

Stellar Evolution
One star’s whole life traced across the H–R diagram, from protostar to white dwarf, brown dwarf or supernova, set by its mass.
Draft
Cluster Ages
A cluster is one age, many masses; the main-sequence turnoff point is its clock. Pleiades → M13.
Draft
Eclipsing Binary
Two stars taking turns hiding each other, with a side-view inset; the dips give their sizes, temperatures and the eclipse type.
Draft
Spectroscopic Binary
One spectral line splitting in two as the stars swing toward and away at inclination i; the speed ratio is the mass ratio.

Galaxies & cosmology

Hubble's Law
v = H₀d from realistic noisy galaxy data and a fit; re-centre on any galaxy, and the implied age of the universe.

Cosmic Distance Ladder
Parallax → main-sequence fitting → Cepheids → Type Ia → Hubble, each rung calibrating the next.
