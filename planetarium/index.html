Date & time
Before 15 Oct 1582 dates are proleptic Gregorian (not the Julian calendar then in use). Year 0 = 1 BC.

Settings

Location

Sky

On: while you follow something and time runs faster than about a day every 17 seconds, day and night, the ground and refraction are not drawn and the view does not spin with the sky. Off: everything is drawn, and flickers as days go by.

Mount

Alt-az keeps the horizon level. Equatorial keeps the celestial pole up, so the sky turns about the pole as time runs. M

Labels & markings

Solar system

On: bodies are drawn where we see them (light that left them minutes ago). Off: where they are at this instant.

Faint stars

On: when you zoom in past magnitude 12, the stars of the field on screen are fetched: to magnitude 17 from this site’s star tiles (stars.sethhopper.com), beyond that from the GAVO Data Center in Heidelberg, which also stands in if the tiles cannot be reached. Only the sky position of the field is sent. Off, or offline: stars to magnitude 12 from the built-in catalogue.
