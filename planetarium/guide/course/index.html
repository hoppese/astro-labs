Earlham College · PHYS 115

Encounters with the Cosmos

Introductory astronomy in two semesters. The first goes from the sky overhead to the size of the solar system; the second from what starlight tells us to the expanding universe. The thread through both is the cosmic distance ladder, and the activities are labs in the planetarium, where every measurement is made by looking at the sky.

How a week runs

  • Three 50-minute classes (Monday, Wednesday, Friday), each opening with a kickoff: a short collaborative Moodle quiz on the reading or the last class.
  • Activities are planetarium labs, done in class: each has a lab guide with background, diagrams and the steps in detail.
  • Homework is due on Fridays (Moodle, two tries per problem).
  • Quizzes take 20 to 30 minutes at the start of a class; a midterm, and a final in exam week. In semester 2, a group project on an underrepresented astronomer or culture.

Weights and counts follow the Fall 2025 syllabus for now (10 of 11 HWs, 30 of 33 kickoffs, activities, 3 quizzes, midterm, final, project); with a planetarium lab most weeks there are more activities than the 6 of Fall 2025, so their weighting needs a decision.
